# PM employer shortlist — Top 56

Updated: 21 October 2026

This is a ranked company watchlist for the agreed PM-related roles, including companies without suitable vacancies today. The ordering is a judgement based primarily on work-life-balance signals and practical location/commuting fit. It is not a verified ranking of team workloads. Published benefits and employee-review awards are evidence signals, not guarantees about a particular manager or project.

## Screening rules

- **First priority: work-life balance.** Check actual working hours, overtime, meeting time zones, workload, leave use and any shifts/on-call obligations.
- **Location:** prefer remote work from Portugal. North Portugal roles should be practical from Santo Tirso; retain the existing target of no more than two office days per week. Porto, Matosinhos, Maia, Vila Nova de Gaia, Braga and nearby Lousado are relevant locations, subject to the real commute.
- **Distant offices:** only consider a remote arrangement with required visits no more often than quarterly.
- **International travel:** **no more than one week abroad in any quarter**. Include client visits, training, onboarding, team retreats and company offsites. Confirm whether the employer's quoted allowance includes travel days. An annual average is not enough to establish compliance with a per-quarter cap.
- A remote label, flexible-hours policy or nearby office does not establish compliance with the travel cap. **Except where a specific allowance is recorded, travel remains unverified.**
- Keep only the agreed role families and added-word variants. Exclude Head, Director, C-suite and equivalent top-responsibility jobs. Engineering Manager and Team Lead remain eligible.
- **No mandatory code writing**, including coding tests or regular software development. Reading and reviewing code are acceptable.
- Screen salary, required experience and mandatory languages for each vacancy. Inclusion here is not confirmation that a particular opening meets the previously discussed salary expectation.
- Explicitly include **Bosch, Vestas and DACHSER**. Vestas is a specific inclusion despite the earlier wind-sector preference.
- Continue excluding **Aptiv, Critical TechWorks, Nordex, Natixis, Critical Manufacturing and Uphold**.

## Practical evidence limits

- Intellias and several other employers have local employee-feedback recognition. These awards measure broader workplace experience and should not be treated as precise workload scores.
- Buffer's published schedule has four scheduled workdays and a flexible fifth day that can include overflow work.
- Doist/Todoist's 40 PTO days and Oyster's 40 PTO days include public/regional holidays.
- Seven.One's 90 office days per year and the Wolters Kluwer example's eight days per month do not guarantee an even two-day-per-week pattern.
- Vestas's five travel days per year apply to the cited Delivery Manager example. Other Vestas positions have much higher travel requirements.
- Körber's cited PLC Project Manager role requires approximately 30% travel and is excluded. The company remains a conditional target for suitable internal IT, product and project roles.
- TestGorilla's country eligibility varies. Portugal has appeared as an exception in some roles; do not assume all openings can employ someone in Portugal.
- Blip was considered because of its flexible attendance policy, but left out in view of the earlier preference away from gambling/betting employers. [Company business focus](https://www.blip.pt/).
- Sketch advertises an annual meetup and GitBook at least two European meetups each year. Neither policy establishes a maximum duration or guarantees the per-quarter travel cap.
- BIZAY was reviewed for this expansion but not selected: its remote four-day, 36-hour schedule is attractive, while employee feedback about management and pay is mixed. [Official careers](https://www.360imprimir.pt/Home/Carreiras); [employee reviews](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/360imprimir-Reviews-E1138704.htm).
- A company can remain on this monitoring list while an individual vacancy is rejected. Unknown attendance or travel requirements must remain pending until clarified.
